## Features

- **Your whole keyboard, live.** Every key lights up as you press it. Draw it flat, in 3D, or in 3D tilted.
- **12 key layouts:** full size, tenkeyless, 60%, 65%, 75%, 96%, 40%, gaming, number pad, arrows, a WASD cluster or the function row.
- **500 keyboard cases and 500 colour styles.** Floating keys or a case around them: slim, low profile, wood, retro, typewriter, rugged and more. Rest the pointer on one to preview it on the real overlay.
- **Press animation, release fade and press counts.**
- **A mouse overlay too,** in its own window: 200 realistic 3D mice in 13 families, 100 colour sets, and the colour your clicks light up in. Customize the body, width, wheel, thumb rest and side buttons.
- **Build your own layout** in the Layout Designer: keys take their real sizes. Rename, edit and delete them in My keyboards.
- **Put it anywhere:** drag the keyboard or the mouse to move it, drag its edges to resize it. Set the opacity, and whether each one stays on top.
- **Hide it for passwords:** one hotkey hides the keyboard and stops reading keys.
- **Hotkeys that work everywhere:** Ctrl+F9 moves the keyboard, Ctrl+Shift+F9 the mouse, Ctrl+F10 hides the keyboard, Ctrl+F11 shows or hides the mouse. All can be changed.
- **72 keyboard languages** printed on the keys, and the app in **54 languages**.

## Download

Get the latest version from the [download page](https://mynarecorder.com/input-visualizer/download) or the [Releases](https://github.com/myna-recorder/Myna-Input-Visualizer/releases) on this page: the **installer** (it installs into Program Files and asks for administrator rights once), or the **standalone** exe that runs with no installation.

When you open the file, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit, and an internet connection when it starts. Sign in with a Myna account, or continue without one.
